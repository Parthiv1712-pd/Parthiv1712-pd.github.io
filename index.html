Questions

Before you install.

Is it free?
Yes. The app is free to download and use. It's supported by ads, which is the only reason there's no price on it.
Where do my downloads go?
Into your device's Downloads or Movies folder, which means they show up in your gallery and any other player you use. You can change the folder in Settings.
Which links work?
Public links to videos you have the right to save — your own uploads, content you've been given permission to keep, and material that's been released for free use. Sites that block downloading in their terms won't work, and that's deliberate.
Do I need to be online to watch?
Only to download. Once the file is on your phone it plays on a plane, on the metro, anywhere.
Does it collect my data?
The app doesn't ask for an account and doesn't upload your links or files. The full details are in the privacy policy below.

Last updated: 1 January 2026

This policy explains what All Video Downloader ("the app") does with your information. It applies to the Android app published by Jiya Infotech on Google Play.

Information the app collects

The app does not require an account and does not ask you for your name, email address, or phone number. Two kinds of information are involved:

  • Information you provide. The links you paste and the files you download. These are processed on your device to complete the download and are not sent to us.
  • Information collected automatically. Standard technical data such as device model, Android version, app version, and crash reports, collected by the third-party services listed below to keep the app working and to show ads.

Permissions and why they're needed

  • Storage / media access — to save downloaded files to your device and let you play them back.
  • Internet and network state — to fetch the video you asked for and to pause or resume when your connection changes.
  • Notifications — to show download progress and tell you when a file is finished.

You can withdraw any of these at any time in Android Settings → Apps → All Video Downloader → Permissions. Some features stop working without them.
